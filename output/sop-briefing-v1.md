# Monthly S&OP Briefing — March 2026 (M4)

## The month in one paragraph

March closed at **$337,288** in revenue, up from **$311,313** in February — an **8.3% month-on-month gain** with **zero declining SKUs** across the portfolio. Growth is broad-based: every SKU grew, with monthly growth rates ranging from 3.8% at the entry tier to 13.2% at the top of the newest products. The problem this month is not demand, it is supply. Six SKUs need purchase orders, four of them urgently, and the fastest-growing items are also the thinnest on stock. Roughly **$97,665/month of revenue sits behind the four REORDER_NOW lines**. The single decision that matters this cycle: release POs for ranks 1–4 now, and slot ranks 5–6 into the same cycle before they become emergencies.

Second-largest exposure and a shorter runway than rank 1 — a premium 500g format we should not be teaching customers to substitute away from.
3. **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** — REORDER_NOW. No order placed; ~1.62 months of runway against a 2-month target while demand grows 12.2%/month. **$17,396/month** exposed. Growth is near-identical across channels (12.3% Shopify, 12.2% Amazon), which makes the trend more credible than a single-channel spike.

No order placed; ~1.42 months of runway against a 2-month target while demand grows 13.2%/month — the fastest growth in the portfolio. **$16,476/month** exposed, and the shortest runway of the four urgent lines. Amazon is pulling hardest at 15.0%/month.

**Place in this cycle (ranks 5–6):**

6. **Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g** — REORDER_SOON. Runway of 3.32 months clears its 3-month target by under a month. The 3-month target reflects a premium price point and longer supplier lead time — which is exactly why this cannot slip to next cycle despite looking healthier on paper than rank 5.

**No action required:** Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 250g, Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 250g, Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 250g, Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g — all covered against their targets.

## Watch list

- **Manuka Honey MGO 100+ 250g** — MONITOR. Stock is healthy (7 months runway vs a 2-month target), so no PO is needed. But the channel picture needs watching: Amazon units fell in the latest month (404 → 388) even though the overall trend is up. Watch channel mix before extrapolating growth on this SKU. Note also that its runway drops from 7.0 to 5.49 months without the order currently in flight — still comfortable.
- **Propolis Tincture 30ml** — MONITOR. Projected to stock out in ~1.2 months, inside the Q2 2026 phase-out window. Demand is still growing 11.9%/month, so this sells down naturally; deprioritized per phase-out policy unless cover drops below 30 days. Re-check at next cycle.
- **The three newest products** — Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g, Bioactive Blend Energy 250g and Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g all launched mid-January 2026, so their trends are measured M2→M4 rather than M1→M4. Growth rates of 11.6–13.2%/month are on a two-interval base and include early-launch lift; treat the M5 projections (589 / 435 / 412 units) as directional and re-forecast next month with a fuller history.
- **Runway without in-flight orders** — several SKUs depend on POs already placed: Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g drops from 3.02 to 1.93 months, Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 250g from 4.37 to 3.12, Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 250g from 4.22 to 3.09, and Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 250g from 5.33 to 3.89. Any supplier delay converts a "no action" into a reorder.

## The one tension

**Propolis Tincture 30ml is growing 11.9%/month on a product we have decided to stop selling.** Under phase-out policy this is the right outcome — the natural sell-down is on track, stock clears in ~1.2 months, and we avoid writing off residual inventory in Q2. But the policy assumes the phase-out date holds. If Q2 2026 slips, a growing SKU with 1.2 months of cover and no order behind it becomes a live stockout, and we will be explaining an out-of-stock on a line that was still gaining customers.

**Decision needed:** confirm the Q2 2026 phase-out date is firm this cycle. If it is, no order — let it run down. If there is any chance of slippage, place a small bridge order now while the lead time still allows it.
