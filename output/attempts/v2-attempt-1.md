## The month in one paragraph

March came in at about $337K, up from roughly $311K in February — growth across every SKU, no decliners anywhere in the portfolio. The problem isn't demand, it's cover. Four SKUs will run out before we can reasonably resupply them, putting roughly $98K a month of revenue at risk. The big honey lines are comfortable; the 500g formats, the new Bioactive Blends and the premium tiers are the thin ones. Two things need naming before anyone extrapolates: MGO 100+ 250g grew overall but lost units on Amazon, and Propolis Tincture is one of our fastest growers while sitting inside a phase-out.

## What sold — and what the trend says

- **Best sellers by revenue:** Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 250g, Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 250g, and Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g. MGO 263+ 250g alone carries about $60K a month of revenue opportunity and is growing around 6.5% a month.
- **Fastest growers:** Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g (~13% a month), Bioactive Blend Energy 250g (~12%), and Propolis Tincture 30ml (~12%). The two Bioactive lines only launched mid-January, so their trend is measured over two months — real, but young.
- **Premium is pulling:** the larger and stronger grades are growing faster than the entry grades. MGO 850+ 500g is up ~10% a month, MGO 1700+ 100g ~10%, MGO 514+ 500g ~9%.
- **One caveat on the entry tier:** Manuka Honey MGO 100+ 250g looks fine at ~3.8% growth, but Amazon units actually fell in the latest month (404 → 388). The total is being held up by Shopify. Don't read that headline as clean growth until we see whether it's a listing, price or ranking issue.

## Stock risk

Four SKUs sit below the 2-month cover target with no order placed:

- **Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g** — under 1.6 months
- **Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g** — about 1.75 months
- **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** — about 1.6 months
- **Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g** — under 1.5 months

Two more are close to the line: **Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g** at about 2.2 months against a 2-month target, and **Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g** at about 3.3 months against its 3-month target (premium price, longer supplier lead time).

Everything else is covered: MGO 263+ 250g (over 5 months), MGO 514+ 250g and MGO 850+ 250g (both over 4 months), Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g (about 3 months), MGO 100+ 250g (about 7 months).

## Reorder decisions (ranked by revenue at stake)

**Approve today — order now:**

1. **Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g** — about $34K a month exposed. Runs dry in under two months while demand grows ~9% a month. Miss it and we lose the highest-value 500g line at the exact moment it's accelerating.
2. **Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g** — about $29K a month exposed. Under 1.6 months of stock, growing ~10% a month. This is our top-of-range format; a gap here also costs us the customers who trade up.
3. **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** — about $17K a month exposed. About 1.6 months left, growing ~12% a month. A stockout on a nine-week-old launch doesn't just cost the month's revenue — it kills the momentum and the review flow we're building.
4. **Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g** — about $16K a month exposed. Under 1.5 months left, our fastest grower at ~13% a month. Same launch-momentum argument, sooner.

Combined, these four put roughly $98K a month at risk, and the losses start inside six weeks — Recovery first, then Energy and MGO 850+ 500g, then MGO 514+ 500g.

**Put in this cycle's PO — not urgent:**

5. **Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g** — about $40K a month of opportunity. Cover is about 2.2 months, clearing the target by under a month. No emergency, but this is a top-three revenue SKU and the next PO belongs in this cycle.
6. **Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g** — about $20K a month of opportunity. About 3.3 months against a 3-month target, and the supplier lead time is long. Order in this cycle or the buffer disappears while we wait.

**No action:** MGO 263+ 250g, MGO 514+ 250g, MGO 850+ 250g, Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g — all comfortably above target.

## Watch list

- **Manuka Honey MGO 100+ 250g** — stock is fine (about 7 months against a 2-month target), so there's no supply decision here. The issue is commercial: Amazon fell 404 → 388 units last month while Shopify grew ~4%. That's a channel signal, not noise. Before we plan on ~3.8% growth continuing, someone should check listing health, buy-box and pricing on Amazon. If the Amazon slide continues, this SKU's forecast is too high and we're sitting on more cover than we think.
- **Propolis Tincture 30ml** — about 1.2 months of stock, no reorder per phase-out policy. Watch the date, not the demand. See below.
- **Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g** — covered at about 3 months today, but growing ~12% a month off a two-month trend line. It will join the reorder list soon; don't be surprised next cycle.

## The one tension worth discussing

**Propolis Tincture 30ml is growing about 12% a month and we're deliberately letting it run out.** It's slated for Q2 2026 phase-out, and policy says don't reorder unless cover drops under 30 days. At about 1.2 months of stock, it's right on that edge. The revenue at stake is small — roughly $7K a month, the smallest number on the page — so the natural sell-down is working as designed and the honest read is: leave it.

What would change the call is the calendar, not the sales. If the Q2 phase-out slips for any reason — supplier transition, replacement product delay, a marketing commitment we've already made — this stops being an elegant sell-down and becomes a stockout on a product we're still selling and still growing. **The decision we need today isn't a PO; it's confirmation that the Q2 phase-out date is firm.** If anyone in the room thinks it might move, we should place a small bridge order now rather than discover the gap in six weeks.
