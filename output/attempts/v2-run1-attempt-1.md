## The month in one paragraph

March closed at about $337K, up from roughly $311K in February — growth across the board, with no declining SKUs. The demand story is healthy; the supply story is not. Six SKUs need a purchase order this cycle, four of them urgently, and the ones running out fastest are the high-value large formats and the new Bioactive line. Several "comfortable" positions are only comfortable because inbound stock is already on the water. The single decision to make today: approve the four REORDER_NOW purchase orders, together covering roughly $98K a month of exposed revenue.

## What sold — and what the trend says

Best sellers by March revenue were **Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 250g**, **Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 250g**, and **Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g** — the core range is doing the heavy lifting, with MGO 263+ 250g alone carrying about $60K a month of opportunity.

The fastest growers are all newer or smaller lines: **Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g** (about 13% a month), **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** (about 12%), and **Propolis Tincture 30ml** (about 12%). The three Bioactive Blends launched mid-January, so their trend is measured over two months only — real momentum, but a short read. Treat the slope as directional, not settled.

Across the honey range, growth rises with potency and size: MGO 100+ 250g is up about 4% a month, while **MGO 850+ 500g** (about 10%) and **MGO 1700+ 100g** (about 10%) are climbing fastest. Customers are trading up, and that is exactly where our stock is thinnest.

## Stock risk

Four SKUs run dry before a normal reorder cycle can refill them:

- **Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g** — under two months of cover
- **Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g** — about a month and a half
- **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** — about a month and a half
- **Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g** — under a month and a half

Two more clear their targets by less than a month: **Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g** (just over two months against a two-month target) and **Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g** (just over three months against a three-month target, reflecting its longer supplier lead time).

Worth naming: much of the apparent comfort elsewhere is inbound stock, not stock on hand. Without the orders already placed, **MGO 263+ 250g** would sit under four months instead of over five, **MGO 514+ 250g** and **MGO 850+ 250g** would each drop to around three months, and **Bioactive Blend Immunity 250g** would fall from three months to under two. If any of those shipments slip, the safe list gets shorter fast.

## Reorder decisions (ranked by revenue at stake)

**1. Manuka Honey MGO 514+ 500g — order now.** Nothing on order, under two months of cover, demand growing about 9% a month. About $34K a month of revenue is exposed the moment it stocks out — which on current runway is inside the next two months.

**2. Manuka Honey MGO 850+ 500g — order now.** No order placed, about a month and a half of cover, growing about 10% a month. Roughly $29K a month at risk, and it hits sooner than SKU 1. **3. Bioactive Blend Energy 250g — order now.** About a month and a half of cover against a two-month target, growing about 12% a month. Around $17K a month at risk. **4. Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g — order now.** The tightest position on the list, under a month and a half, and the fastest grower we have at about 13% a month. About $16K a month at risk, starting first. Same launch logic as Energy.

**5. Manuka Honey MGO 263+ 500g — order this cycle.** Just over two months of cover against a two-month target. No emergency, but about $40K a month sits behind it — the largest revenue base of anything needing a PO. Put it in this cycle's order, not next.

**6. Manuka Honey MGO 1700+ 100g — order this cycle.** Just over three months against a three-month target, and the longer supplier lead time means "just over" disappears quickly. About $20K a month behind it. Cheap insurance to order now.

## The one tension worth discussing

**Propolis Tincture 30ml is growing about 12% a month and we are phasing it out in Q2.** Stock runs out in roughly five weeks, which lands inside the phase-out window — so on paper this is a clean natural sell-down and policy says don't reorder unless cover drops below 30 days. That's the right call *if the phase-out date holds*.

What would change the call: any slip in the Q2 timeline. The moment phase-out moves, this stops being an orderly wind-down and becomes a stockout on a growing product, with about $7K a month of revenue quietly walking out the door and customers finding a replacement elsewhere. It's the smallest number on this page, which is exactly why it will be the one nobody is watching.

The decision we need today isn't about the tincture's inventory — it's confirmation that the Q2 phase-out date is firm. If it is, we do nothing. If it's uncertain, we need a small bridge order before cover falls under 30 days.
