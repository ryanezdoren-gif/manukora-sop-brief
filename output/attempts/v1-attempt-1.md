# Monthly S&OP Briefing — March 2026 (M4)
**Prepared for:** Manukora US Operations & Leadership
**Scope:** 12 SKUs, Shopify + Amazon combined

---

## 1. Executive Summary

March closed at **$337,288** in revenue, up **8.3%** from February's $311,313. Growth is broad-based: **every SKU in the portfolio grew month-over-month, and there are no decliners.** The portfolio is healthy on the demand side and tight on the supply side — the core issue this cycle is that our fastest-growing SKUs are also our thinnest on stock.

**The one thing to action this week:** four SKUs need a purchase order placed immediately (**MGO 514+ 500g, MGO 850+ 500g, Bioactive Energy, Bioactive Recovery**). Together they carry **~$97,700/month of revenue exposure** and all run dry inside 1.8 months against a 2-month cover target.

**The strategic read:** demand is skewing toward higher-potency and larger formats, and the Bioactive Blend launch is outperforming. Our inventory position was built for the old mix. The reorder list below is really a request to re-baseline purchasing against where the business is actually going.

## 3. What Sold Poorly

**Nothing declined this month.** The weakest performers are weak only in *relative* terms:

- **MGO 100+ 250g — the laggard, and the one genuine soft spot.** Growth of **3.75%/mo** is less than half the portfolio average, and it is the only SKU with a channel-level warning: **Amazon units fell in the latest month (404 → 388)** even though the blended trend is up. Shopify (4.3%) is carrying the SKU while Amazon stalls. This is our entry-level price point, so a soft Amazon signal here may be an acquisition-funnel issue rather than a demand issue — worth a look from marketing, not just ops.
- **Propolis Tincture 30ml** is the smallest revenue line at $6,578/month. Its "poor" status is a deliberate portfolio decision (Q2 phase-out), not a performance failure — in fact it is growing at 11.9%/mo.
- **MGO 1700+ 100g** is modest in absolute revenue ($19,857/month) but that is expected for a 100g halo product at the top of the range; 10.2%/mo growth is healthy.

---

## 4. Trends to Watch

**a) Premiumisation is real and accelerating.** Growth rate rises almost monotonically with MGO grade:

- MGO 100+ → 3.8%
- MGO 263+ → 6.5–7.2%
- MGO 514+ → 7.3–9.3%
- MGO 850+ → 6.8–9.9%
- MGO 1700+ → 10.2%

Customers are trading up. Purchasing and marketing plans built on the current volume mix will under-serve the top of the range.

**b) Large formats outgrow small formats within every tier.** In both matched pairs, 500g beats 250g:

- MGO 263+: 500g at 7.2% vs 250g at 6.5%
- MGO 514+: 500g at 9.3% vs 250g at 7.3%
- MGO 850+: 500g at 9.9% vs 250g at 6.8%

Basket sizes are growing. Note that **both 500g SKUs in the 514+/850+ tiers are also our two most urgent reorders** — we are actively under-stocked precisely where growth is strongest.

**c) The Bioactive Blend launch is working.** All three variants launched mid-January and are compounding at 11.6–13.2%/mo (trend measured M2→M4, so treat these rates as early-life and likely to moderate). Combined, they now represent **1,280 units and ~$57,400/month of opportunity** — already comparable to our #1 SKU. Recovery is the growth leader (13.2%) despite being the smallest of the three.

**d) Amazon is outgrowing Shopify on premium and new products.** Amazon leads on MGO 850+ 500g (11.6% vs 8.7%), MGO 1700+ (12.1% vs 9.0%), Bioactive Recovery (15.0% vs 12.1%) and Propolis (13.3% vs 11.0%). Shopify leads mainly on MGO 514+ 250g and Bioactive Energy. The exception is MGO 100+, where Amazon actively declined. **Read: Amazon is becoming our premium discovery channel, not our entry channel** — a notable inversion of the usual assumption, and one that should inform channel-level assortment and ad spend.

### 🟠 Watch — clears target, but by less than a month

| Priority | SKU | Runway | Target | Monthly Exposure |

MGO 263+ 500g is the largest revenue line in this group — a slipped PO here costs more than either of the top-two critical items. MGO 1700+ has the longest lead time in the portfolio, so its 0.32-month buffer is thinner in practice than it looks.

### 🟡 Special case — Propolis Tincture 30ml

Runway of **1.2 months** — the shortest in the portfolio — but deliberately **not reordered** under phase-out policy (reorder only if cover falls below 30 days). Demand is still growing 11.9%/mo, so the sell-down is tracking as intended.

> **Tension to flag:** we are running a growing product to zero on purpose. If the Q2 2026 phase-out date slips even a few weeks, this converts from a clean exit into an unplanned stockout on a still-accelerating SKU. **Ask: confirm the phase-out date with commercial before the next cycle.** The financial stake is small ($6,578/month), but the customer-experience and Amazon listing-health cost of an unmanaged stockout is not.

### 🟢 Comfortable

MGO 263+ 250g (5.33 mo), MGO 514+ 250g (4.37 mo), MGO 850+ 250g (4.22 mo), Bioactive Immunity (3.02 mo) — all clear target with room. **MGO 100+ 250g at 7.0 months is over-covered** against a 2-month target; combined with its 3.8% growth and soft Amazon signal, this is the one place we may be carrying more inventory than the demand justifies.

## 6. Reorder Recommendations

### Place now (this week)

| Priority | SKU | Why | Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **MGO 514+ 500g** | Runs out in ~1.75 months against a 2-month target while demand grows 9.3%/mo. Highest revenue at risk of the critical four. | $34,316/mo |
| **2** | **MGO 850+ 500g** | 1.58 months of cover, 9.9%/mo growth, no PO placed. Premium tier — margin impact exceeds unit impact. | $29,477/mo |
| **3** | **Bioactive Blend Energy 250g** | 1.62 months of cover against 12.2%/mo growth. A stockout 10 weeks after launch damages the product's trajectory, not just the month. | $17,396/mo |
| **4** | **Bioactive Blend Recovery 250g** | Shortest runway of the actionable set (1.42 mo) and the fastest grower in the portfolio (13.2%/mo). Amazon growth of 15.0% is running ahead of plan. | $16,476/mo |

**Reasoning behind the ranking:** priority is set by revenue at risk, with runway as the tiebreaker. Note that ranks 3 and 4 have *shorter* runway and *faster* growth than ranks 1 and 2 — they rank lower purely on dollar value. Given both are newly launched, **there is a case for treating all four as a single order block rather than a sequence.** Early-life stockouts suppress review velocity and search ranking on Amazon, and that damage outlasts the missed month of revenue. If supplier constraints force a choice, protect the two Bioactive lines on strategic grounds even though the honey SKUs are worth more today.

### Place this cycle (no emergency)

| Priority | SKU | Why | Exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5** | **MGO 263+ 500g** | 2.22 months of cover clears the 2-month target by under a month. Next PO belongs in this cycle. | $40,307/mo |
| **6** | **MGO 1700+ 100g** | 3.32 months against a 3-month target. Longer supplier lead time means the order date matters more than the runway number suggests. | $19,857/mo |

**Note:** MGO 263+ 500g carries the *highest* dollar exposure of any SKU on the reorder list ($40,307/mo) — more than priority 1. It ranks lower only because it currently clears its cover target. It should not be allowed to slip to next cycle.

### Monitor, no order

- **MGO 100+ 250g** — 7 months of cover against a 2-month target. No supply action needed. The real question is commercial: **why did Amazon units fall 404 → 388 while Shopify grew 4.3%?** Confirm whether this is a listing, pricing, competitive or ad-spend issue before we extrapolate the blended 3.8% growth rate into next quarter's buy. Do not build the next MGO 100+ PO on the current trendline.
- **Propolis Tincture 30ml** — hold per phase-out policy; confirm the Q2 date (see Section 5).

### No action

MGO 263+ 250g, MGO 514+ 250g, MGO 850+ 250g, Bioactive Blend Immunity — all comfortably covered.

## 7. Decisions Requested

1. **Approve the four critical POs** (priorities 1–4, ~$97,700/month of exposure) — this week, ideally as one order block.
2. **Approve priorities 5–6** for placement within this cycle; MGO 263+ 500g is the largest single revenue line on the list.
3. **Confirm the Propolis Q2 phase-out date** so we can either let it run out cleanly or bring it back into the reorder plan.
4. **Assign an owner to the MGO 100+ Amazon decline** — this is the only genuine demand-side warning in the portfolio.
5. **Re-baseline the standing buy plan toward premium and 500g formats.** The 3.8%-to-13.2% growth spread means our current stock allocation is fighting the trend; three of our four critical shortages are in exactly the segments growing fastest.
